## The Findings

**It's wealth, not population.** The richest 16% of the world produces over 80% of emissions. Population growth? Weak correlation. Economic output? Strong.

**Energy is the lever.** 76% of emissions come from energy alone. Solar grew 15x last decade. Wind grew 8x. The tech exists—it's policy now.

**History matters.** US: ~25% of all emissions since 1800. China leads today. Climate responsibility is shared but unequal.
