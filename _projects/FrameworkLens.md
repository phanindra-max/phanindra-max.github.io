FrameworkLens is a lightweight survey app that scores readiness across **NIST AI RMF**, **COSO ERM**, and core **GRC** practices. It is designed for quick demos and stakeholder discussions, not formal assessments.

---

## What It Does

**Three-part scoring** — Separate readiness scores for NIST AI RMF, COSO ERM, and GRC fundamentals.  
**Fast feedback** — Lightweight survey flow for rapid, repeatable assessments.  
**Clear positioning** — Intended for demos and conversations rather than compliance audits.

**Built With:** Python • Streamlit
